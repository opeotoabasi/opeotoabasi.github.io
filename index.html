Why trading technology

I used to be a trader. What kept pulling me in was never only the market. It was the machinery under the screen: how an order is accepted, queued by price and time, matched and reported back, and how fast a system can do that without getting it wrong.

Trading showed me an order book from the user's side. Building one shows me the other side, where every choice in the data structures, the concurrency and the networking affects speed and correctness. That is why I wrote a matching engine in C++, and why I want to work on trading and settlement infrastructure.
