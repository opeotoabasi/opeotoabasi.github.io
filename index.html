My story

Before I was 10, I was building small hardware and electrical projects. At about 11 I met BASIC, the first language I used in class, and wrote my first program, a Monopoly game. Moving from hardware to software has shaped how I approach everything since: I want to know how the machine really works.

I also used to be a trader. What kept me interested was the machinery under the screen: how an order is accepted, queued by price and time, matched and reported back, and how fast a system can do that without getting it wrong. Trading showed me an order book from the user's side, so I decided to build one from the other side. That is the matching engine below, and it is why I want to work on trading and infrastructure software.
